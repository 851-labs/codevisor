The Gmail plugin connects a Google account you choose to Codevisor, so your agents can work in that
account's mail: find and read conversations, draft replies, send messages you approve, and keep the
inbox organized. An agent can also wait for new mail and act when it arrives.

<Callout title="Preview">
  The Gmail plugin is in limited preview and isn't in the plugin catalog for everyone yet.
</Callout>

## What agents can do

| Tool                           | What it does                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `search_messages`              | Search messages with Gmail search syntax, such as `from:ana is:unread` |
| `search_threads`               | Search conversations, one result per thread                            |
| `read_message`, `read_thread`  | Read a message, or a whole conversation oldest first                   |
| `list_labels`                  | List the account's labels                                              |
| `list_send_as`                 | List the addresses and aliases the account can send from               |
| `create_draft`, `update_draft` | Write a new message or a reply as a draft, and change it               |
| `send`, `reply`                | Send a message, a reply, or a draft, only after you approve it         |
| `archive`                      | Move messages or conversations out of the inbox                        |
| `mark_read`, `mark_unread`     | Change read state                                                      |
| `modify_labels`                | Add or remove labels                                                   |
| `create_label`, `update_label` | Create a label, or rename it and change its color or visibility        |
| `delete_label`                 | Delete a label; the mail under it stays                                |

The **message.received** event fires when a new message arrives in the inbox. An agent subscribed
to it, optionally with a filter such as `from` or `subject`, wakes up with the new message's
summary. See [plugin events](/docs/plugins#events).

Agents draft by default. Before `send` or `reply`, an agent shows you the exact sender, recipients,
subject, and body and waits for your explicit approval; the tools refuse to send without it.

Agents can send from any **Send mail as** address set up in Gmail's settings, such as an alias on
another domain. Like Gmail, a reply goes out from the address the message was sent to, and a new
message from the account's default address unless you ask for another one.

## Connect an account

1. In Codevisor, open **Settings › Plugins**, choose **Browse Plugins…**, and install **Gmail**.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google**. Google's consent screen opens in your browser.
3. Pick the account and review the permissions. Codevisor asks for:
   - **Read, compose, and send emails from your Gmail account** (`gmail.modify`), for the tools
     above.
   - **See your primary Google Account email address** (`email`, `openid`), to label the account
     and route its new-mail notifications.

`gmail.modify` is the narrowest single Google scope that covers search, reading full messages,
drafts, sending, managing labels, and label changes such as archiving and marking read. It can't
delete mail permanently, and the plugin has no tool that deletes mail.

One sign-in works on every Mac signed in to your Codevisor account. You can connect more than one
Google account; agents choose one per tool call.

## Where your mail goes

The plugin runs on your own computer and talks to Gmail directly over Google's API. Messages,
drafts, and labels are fetched and processed there. Codevisor's servers never receive your email.

- **Your AI provider.** Mail an agent reads becomes part of that chat. Your computer sends it to the
  AI provider you chose for the agent, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, so the agent can do what you
  asked. That provider's terms govern it.
- **Your chat history.** Chats are stored on your computer. When you open a chat from another of
  your devices, it travels through Codevisor Cloud end-to-end encrypted between your devices.
- **New-mail notifications.** Gmail sends Codevisor Cloud a notice through Google Cloud Pub/Sub that
  contains only the account's email address and a mailbox change number, never message content.
  Cloud looks up your computers by a hash of the address, forwards the notice to them encrypted,
  and your computers fetch the new message from Gmail themselves.
- **Sign-in tokens.** Google's tokens are stored in Codevisor's credential vault, encrypted on your
  computer with a key only your devices hold. Codevisor Cloud keeps an encrypted copy so your Macs
  can share one sign-in, and it can't decrypt it.

Codevisor does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its
use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. The [privacy policy](/privacy#google) covers Google user
data in full.

## Disconnect

Choose **Sign Out** for the account in Gmail's plugin settings, or uninstall the plugin. Codevisor
stops new-mail notifications for the account, deletes its sign-in tokens, and removes the account's
inbox and address hash from Cloud. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google
Account's [third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) page.
