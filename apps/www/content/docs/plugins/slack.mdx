The Slack plugin connects your Slack account to Codevisor, so your agents can work in your
workspace: find and read conversations, post or reply when you approve it, react to messages, and
share files. An agent can also wait for something to happen in Slack and act on it.

<Callout title="Preview">
  The Slack plugin is in preview and isn't in the plugin catalog for everyone yet.
</Callout>

## What agents can do

| Tool                               | What it does                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `search_messages`                  | Search messages with Slack search syntax, such as `in:#eng from:@ana has:link` |
| `search_files`                     | Search files shared in your conversations                                      |
| `list_channels`                    | List the channels, private channels, DMs, and group DMs you're in              |
| `find_users`, `get_user`           | Find people by name, handle, or email, and read a profile                      |
| `read_channel`, `read_thread`      | Read a conversation's recent messages, or a whole thread                       |
| `list_channel_members`             | List the people in a conversation                                              |
| `download_file`                    | Save a shared file on your computer                                            |
| `add_reaction`, `remove_reaction`  | React to a message with an emoji                                               |
| `post_message`, `schedule_message` | Post or schedule a message or a thread reply, only after you approve it        |
| `upload_file`                      | Share a file from your computer, only after you approve it                     |

Everything an agent posts appears under your name. Before it posts, schedules, or shares a file, an
agent shows you the exact text and where it goes and waits for your approval; the tools refuse to
post without it.

## Events

Agents can subscribe to these events, usually with a filter, and wake up when they fire. See
[plugin events](/docs/plugins#events).

| Event             | Fires when                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mention.created` | Someone @-mentions you                                    |
| `dm.received`     | Someone sends you a DM, or writes in a group DM you're in |
| `thread.replied`  | Someone replies in a thread you started                   |
| `message.posted`  | Someone posts in any conversation you're in               |
| `reaction.added`  | Someone reacts to one of your messages                    |

Message events carry the text, the channel's name and type, the author, and a link to the message,
so filters such as `channel.name == "incidents" && !bot` work. Your own messages never trigger
events.

## Connect your workspace

Codevisor reaches Slack through a small app that you create in your own workspace, so no one else's
app gets access to your messages. Codevisor walks you through it when you install the plugin:

1. In Codevisor, open **Settings › Plugins**, choose **Browse Plugins…**, and install **Slack**.
   The **Connect Slack** setup opens.
2. Choose **Create Slack App**. Slack opens with the app's settings filled in. Pick your workspace,
   choose **Next**, then **Create and Install**, and **Allow** the install.
3. When Slack says **Codevisor is ready!**, choose **Go to App Settings**. Under **App Credentials**,
   choose **Show** next to **Signing Secret**, copy it, and paste it into Codevisor.
4. Open **OAuth & Permissions** in the sidebar, copy the **User OAuth Token** (it starts with
   `xoxp-`), and paste it into Codevisor.
5. Choose **Connect**. Codevisor checks the token with Slack and names the account after your
   workspace.

If your workspace only allows approved apps, Slack sends the install to an admin first; finish
setup after they approve it. You can connect more than one workspace; agents choose one per tool call.

The app asks Slack for these permissions, all on your behalf: reading the channels, private
channels, DMs, and group DMs you're in (`channels:history`, `groups:history`, `im:history`,
`mpim:history` and their `:read` scopes), opening DMs (`im:write`), posting (`chat:write`),
reading and adding reactions (`reactions:read`, `reactions:write`), reading and sharing files
(`files:read`, `files:write`), searching (`search:read`), and looking people up (`users:read`,
`users:read.email`). It also subscribes to message and reaction events in your conversations.

## Where your messages go

The plugin runs on your own computer and talks to Slack directly over Slack's API.

- **Your AI provider.** Messages an agent reads become part of that chat. Your computer sends them
  to the AI provider you chose for the agent so it can do what you asked. That provider's terms
  govern it.
- **Events.** Slack sends each event, including the message text, to your account's Codevisor Cloud
  inbox. Cloud encrypts it for your devices as it arrives and holds only that encrypted copy until
  they take it, so a sleeping Mac catches up when it wakes. Your computer checks Slack's signature
  before using an event.
- **Your token and signing secret** are stored in Codevisor's credential vault, encrypted on your
  computer with a key only your devices hold.

## Disconnect

Remove the account in Slack's plugin settings, or uninstall the plugin. Codevisor deletes the token
and signing secret and removes the account's inbox. To revoke access in Slack too, delete the app
from your workspace at [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps).
